Eight time zones, four tools, and nobody knows which doc is current.

Remote teams don't lack communication. They lack one place where it ends up.

  • “Wait, when did we decide that?”

    The decision is in a thread from three weeks ago. Or it was said on a call and never written down.

  • “Can we find a time that works?”

    Someone always takes the 6am slot, and status meetings use up the only overlap you have.

  • “Which version is the latest?”

    Specs in one tool, the roadmap in another, and the newest hire has no idea where to start.

What Tandem does

Three things, done properly: written standups, a record of decisions, and a roadmap that reflects what's actually happening.

Standups you post when you start work, not when everyone else does.

Everyone posts an update at the start of their own day. Tandem collects them into a morning digest for each person and sends blockers to whoever can fix them.

  • Post in text, voice or a short video
  • Digests arrive in each person's own morning
  • Anything marked blocked goes to its owner

Common questions

Anything else: help@tandem.app

Does Tandem replace Slack or Jira?

No. Keep chatting in Slack and tracking tickets in Jira or Linear. Tandem holds specs, decisions and plans, and syncs both ways so nothing gets entered twice.

How long does setup take?

Most teams run their first async standup within 15 minutes. Existing docs import from Notion, Confluence or Google Drive.

What happens when my trial ends?

You move to the free Starter plan. Nothing is deleted, and you can upgrade whenever you like.

Is my data secure?

Tandem is SOC 2 Type II compliant, encrypts data in transit and at rest, and offers EU or US data residency on Enterprise.

Do you discount for startups or non-profits?

Yes. Early-stage startups and registered non-profits get 50% off the Team plan for the first year.
