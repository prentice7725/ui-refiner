Remote work broke the way product teams talk

When your team spans eight time zones, context gets scattered across chat, docs, tickets and meetings nobody can attend.

  • Wait, when did we decide that?

    Key decisions get buried in a thread from three weeks ago — or were never written down at all.

  • Can we find a time that works?

    Someone always takes the 6am call. Status meetings eat the only overlap hours you have.

  • Which doc is the latest?

    Specs live in four tools, roadmaps in a fifth, and the newest hire has no idea where to look.

One home for the work between the work

Tandem connects the conversations, decisions and plans that turn ideas into shipped products.

Questions teams ask before switching

Does Tandem replace Slack or Jira?

No — it connects them. Keep chatting in Slack and tracking tickets in Jira or Linear. Tandem is where specs, decisions and plans live, with two-way sync so nothing gets duplicated.

How long does setup take?

Most teams are running their first async standup within 15 minutes. You can import existing docs from Notion, Confluence or Google Drive in a few clicks.

What happens when my free trial ends?

You'll move to the free Starter plan automatically. Nothing is deleted, and you can upgrade any time.

Is my data secure?

Tandem is SOC 2 Type II compliant, encrypts data in transit and at rest, and offers EU or US data residency on Enterprise plans.

Do you offer discounts for startups or non-profits?

Yes. Eligible early-stage startups and registered non-profits get 50% off the Team plan for the first year.
