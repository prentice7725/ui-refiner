Getting food is simple

Our Main Pantry is a client-choice market — you shop for the groceries your household actually wants, including fresh produce, dairy, protein, and pantry staples. Everyone is welcome — bring a bag and we'll take care of the rest.

  • No appointment needed.Just come during open hours.
  • No proof of income required.We'll ask for your name and household size — that's it.
  • Visit once a week.Plus fresh produce any day we're open.
  • Can't make it in?Seniors and people with disabilities can request home delivery.

Neighbors helping neighbors

Riverbend Community Food Bank started in a church basement nearly 40 years ago. Serving the Riverbend area since 1987 — today we're the hub of a network that reaches every corner of the county.

  • 1.8Mmeals shared with families across Riverbend County last year
  • 14,500people served each month
  • 32partner pantries & meal sites
  • 94¢of every dollar goes to programs

Our programs

Hunger looks different for everyone. These programs meet people where they are.

  • Families

    Main Pantry Market

    A grocery-store style pantry where households choose their own fresh and shelf-stable foods.

  • Kids

    Weekend Backpacks

    Kid-friendly meals sent home every Friday with students at 18 local schools.

  • Seniors

    Home Delivery

    Volunteers bring monthly grocery boxes to older adults and neighbors who can't travel.

  • Neighborhoods

    Mobile Pantry

    Our truck brings fresh produce to rural towns and apartment complexes twice a week.

Your gift fills plates right here at home

Every $1 you give helps provide 3 meals. Because we partner with farms, grocers, and regional food networks, every dollar stretches far. Gifts are tax-deductible — Riverbend Community Food Bank is a 501(c)(3) non-profit.
