Getting food

The Main Pantry is set up like a small market. You walk the aisles and choose your own produce, dairy, meat and pantry staples.

  • No appointment needed. Come any time during open hours.
  • No proof of income. We ask for your name and household size, and that's all.
  • Once a week for a full shop, plus fresh produce any day we're open.
  • Can't get here? Seniors and people with disabilities can ask for home delivery.

420 River Road, Riverbend
Free parking out back. The entrance and aisles are wheelchair accessible.

Hablamos español. We can arrange an interpreter for other languages, just ask at the desk or call (555) 555-0142.

About us

We started in a church basement on Elm Street in 1987. Today the food bank supplies a network of pantries and meal sites that reaches every town in the county.

Programs

Main Pantry Market Families

A grocery-store style pantry where households choose their own fresh and shelf-stable foods.

Weekend Backpacks Kids

Kid-friendly meals sent home every Friday with students at 18 local schools.

Home Delivery Seniors

Volunteers bring a grocery box once a month to older adults and neighbors who can't travel.

Mobile Pantry Rural towns & apartments

Our truck brings fresh produce out to rural towns and apartment complexes twice a week.

Donate

We buy in bulk with farms, grocers and the regional food network, so a dollar goes a long way here. Riverbend Community Food Bank is a 501(c)(3) non-profit and gifts are tax-deductible.

  • Drop off foodMost needed right now: peanut butter, canned meat or fish, cereal, cooking oil and diapers. Loading dock, Mon–Sat.
  • Run a food driveAt work, school or your congregation. We'll bring the bins and signs.
