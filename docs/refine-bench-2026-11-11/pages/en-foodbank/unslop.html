Getting food is simple

Our Main Pantry is a client-choice market. You shop for the groceries your household actually wants, including fresh produce, dairy, protein and pantry staples.

  • No appointment needed. Just come during open hours.
  • No proof of income required. We'll ask for your name and household size, and that's it.
  • Visit once a week. Plus fresh produce any day we're open.
  • Can't make it in? Seniors and people with disabilities can request home delivery.

Our programs

Hunger looks different for everyone. These programs meet people where they are.

For families

Main Pantry Market

A grocery-store style pantry where households choose their own fresh and shelf-stable foods.

For kids

Weekend Backpacks

Kid-friendly meals sent home every Friday with students at 18 local schools.

For seniors

Home Delivery

Volunteers bring monthly grocery boxes to older adults and neighbors who can't travel.

For neighborhoods

Mobile Pantry

Our truck brings fresh produce to rural towns and apartment complexes twice a week.

Your gift fills plates right here at home

Because we partner with farms, grocers and regional food networks, every dollar stretches far. Gifts are tax-deductible. Riverbend Community Food Bank is a 501(c)(3) non-profit.

  • Drop off food. Most needed: peanut butter, canned protein, cereal, cooking oil and diapers. Mon–Sat at the loading dock.
  • Host a food drive at your workplace, school or place of worship. We'll provide bins and signs.
  • Share your harvest. Gardeners and farmers can donate fresh produce.
