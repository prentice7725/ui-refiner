A neighborhood practice, not a dental chain

We've been on Maple Street since 2008. We're independently owned, so our dentists — not a corporate office — decide how long your appointment takes.

  1. Unhurried appointments

    Hygiene visits are a full hour, so there's time to answer your questions.

  2. Clear, upfront pricing

    You'll get a written estimate before any treatment — no surprise bills.

  3. Whole-family scheduling

    Book everyone back-to-back in one trip, including early-morning and Saturday slots.
