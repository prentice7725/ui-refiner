Services

Most families book everyone back-to-back in one trip. Ask the front desk to line it up.

  • Cleanings & exams

    Hour-long hygiene visits, low-radiation digital X-rays, and an oral cancer screening at every checkup.

  • Kids

    First-tooth visits, sealants, fluoride, and sports mouthguards. There's a treasure chest at the front desk, and it's not optional.

  • Fillings, crowns & implants

    Tooth-colored fillings, bridges, root canals, and implants. Most crowns are milled here and fitted the same day.

  • Whitening & aligners

    Professional whitening, porcelain veneers, and clear aligners.

  • Emergencies

    Toothache, chipped tooth, lost filling. Call before 11 am for a same-day slot. After hours, press 2 to reach the dentist on call.

  • If you're nervous

    Nitrous oxide, noise-cancelling headphones, a weighted blanket, and longer appointments so nobody's in a hurry. Just mention it when you book.
