FAQ

Questions, answered

Is my financial data safe?

Yes. Bank connections are read-only, so Pennywise can never move money. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and we never sell it or show you ads.

Do I have to connect my bank?

No. You can add transactions manually or import a CSV file. Bank sync is optional and available on the Plus plan.

What happens when my trial ends?

You choose: subscribe to Plus, or drop to the free Basic plan and keep all your data. We'll remind you three days before the trial ends.

Can I use Pennywise with my partner?

Yes. Shared budgets let you split some categories while keeping others private. Both people get full access on a single Plus subscription.

Which devices does it work on?

iPhone, iPad, Android and any modern web browser. Everything syncs automatically.
