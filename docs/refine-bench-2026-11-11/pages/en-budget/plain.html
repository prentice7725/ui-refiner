Common questions

Is my financial data safe?

Bank connections are read-only, so Pennywise can't move money. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and we don't sell it or show you ads.

Do I have to connect my bank?

No. You can add transactions by hand or import a CSV. Bank sync is optional and part of Plus.

What happens when my trial ends?

You pick: subscribe to Plus, or move to Basic and keep everything. We'll email you three days before the trial ends.

Can I use Pennywise with my partner?

Yes. Split some categories and keep others private. One Plus subscription covers both of you.

Which devices does it work on?

iPhone, iPad, Android and any modern browser. Changes sync between them automatically.

Give it a month.

If it doesn't help, switch to the free plan and keep your data. No card needed to start.
