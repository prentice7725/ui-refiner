# Korean and Japanese sites by trade

What human-made Korean and Japanese sites of one trade do on their first screen, measured in November 2026. `ui-refiner directions deal --trade <trade>` uses `human.json` for the familiar direction.

- `human.json`: per trade and country (for example `salon/JP`), the share of each hero structure, the first-screen ground and accent family, and type (median body size, largest text over body, body line height on Japanese pages, share of sites with a serif or Mincho display face). A group appears only with 8 or more sites; 41 groups from 882 sites. Built by `scripts/build-genre-reference.mjs`.
- `sites.tsv`, `sites.json`, `summary.json`, `SUMMARY.md`: the full scan of 996 sites (`scripts/collect-reference.mjs`).
- `colour/`: first-screen colours of 1017 sites (`scripts/collect-colours.mjs`).

The list is `scripts/genre-sites.tsv`: Japanese sites from a hand list and the industry pages of MUUUUU.ORG, Korean sites from a hand list, and Korean dermatology, dental and Korean medicine clinics from the homepage column of the Health Insurance Review and Assessment Service's "전국 병의원 및 약국 현황" (September 2026, data.go.kr, KOGL type 1: source: 건강보험심사평가원). Clinics were picked one per host in a fixed order, at most half from Seoul, Gyeonggi and Incheon; about a third of the listed addresses no longer answered. Japanese dermatology and dental clinics come from the homepage column of the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare's 医療情報ネット open data (1 June 2026), and Japanese care providers (day services, fee-charging homes, special nursing homes) from the URL column of its 介護サービス情報公表システム open data (end of June 2026); both under 公共データ利用規約 第1.0版 (source: 厚生労働省). They were picked the same way, at most half from Tokyo, Kanagawa, Saitama and Chiba; about nine in ten addresses answered. Small Korean and Japanese businesses (credit unions, inns, makers, bakeries, dental clinics, software firms, design and architecture offices) were added on 18 November 2026 from a web-search candidate list; each homepage was opened and its title checked, dead hosts, robots-blocked hosts and one already listed were left out, and inner pages were replaced by the homepage. robots.txt was respected and only measurements are kept. A site counts only when its page declares the country's language, which also drops a few real ones. Groups are small (8–67 sites), so read the shares as tendencies. Korean hair salons, gyms, wedding halls, tax offices, estate agents, shops, cosmetics, games, restaurants and design offices have fewer than 8 sites and no group.
