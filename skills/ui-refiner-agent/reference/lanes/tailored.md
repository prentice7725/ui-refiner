# Tailored

The direction made for this one client: its colour, its type and its look are decided from the client's own content, and nothing is handed over ready-made. This is the method of benchmark round 12. In the round-18 pilot it gave the most varied sets of pages (first of all arms in every set judgement) and ranked at or near the top page by page.

Read [README.md](README.md) first; this file adds what is particular to this lane.

## What is dealt

- A hero structure.
- A surface: plain white, a brand colour ground, a paper ground or a dark ground.
- Nothing else. Colour and type are yours to choose, apart from the faces to avoid.

## How to build it

- **Start from the content, not from a style.** Write the direction as one line taken from what this client does and says, never a style word such as "minimal and elegant". Then decide what carries the first screen: the product, a photograph, data or the type itself. Never a gradient.
- **Type.** Choose it yourself: one strong display face picked for this subject and this language, pushed through the whole page, with a text face beside it. Set it at a clear size contrast (the display about three times the body on a persuasive page; a Japanese or Korean page may stay smaller), use one family for every figure, and keep body text at 16px or more. In round 11 the pages that pushed one strong face of their own were ranked first in 21 of 24 page rankings; pages built from safe pairings were called plain.
- **Colour.** Take the palette from the client: what it sells, where it is, what its own material looks like. One accent, used for the main action and the signature element. On a brand ground, the ground is the colour and the accent is quiet.
- **Surface.** Build on the dealt surface. A real brand surface overrides it. A paper ground is a clear paper colour, not a faint off-white; a dark ground is carried by a photograph or by type, never by a dashboard of metric cards with a neon accent.
- **The signature element** is the most memorable thing on the page here. Build it from the page's real content, at the size that content needs, beside or below the headline and never in larger type than it.
- **The whole page differs in rhythm.** No three sections in a row share one layout; widths, density and alignment change with the content.

## What this lane is not

It is not the generated look with the tells removed, and it is not another stock look in its place: cream paper with a serif display face and tracked labels, or a white page with thin rules and one blue accent. If a rival's name could go on the mock and nothing would look wrong, push the type, the colour or the signature further.
